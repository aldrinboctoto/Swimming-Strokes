Community Impact

Significance to the Swimming Community

AquaVision AI brings joyful, accessible computer vision to local pools, swim clubs, and beginner swimmers—helping everyone build confidence in the water without costly gear.

Engaging Youth & Beginner Learning

Learning to swim is even more exciting when technology feels interactive and encouraging. Swim instructors and youth programs can use AquaVision AI as a fun poolside visual aid to recognize strokes, equipment, or water activities in real time.

Impact: Turns swim practice into interactive discovery

Zero-Cost Access for Local Clubs

High-end sports analytics usually require expensive underwater rigs and paid subscriptions. Hosted freely on GitHub Pages, AquaVision AI runs on any everyday smartphone, tablet, or laptop so community pools can benefit immediately.

Impact: Removes financial & hardware barriers

Family-Safe On-Device Privacy

Privacy around swimming pools is essential. Because TensorFlow.js runs the neural network strictly inside the user's local browser, zero video frames or photos are ever uploaded to external cloud servers.

Impact: Complete peace of mind for families & coaches
